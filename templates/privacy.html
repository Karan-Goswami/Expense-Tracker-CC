Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. You can delete your expenses or your account at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@spendly.example.
